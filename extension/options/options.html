How it works

Three actions that are easy to confuse.

Add
Puts someone on the campaign's lead list and enrols them. The contact is created first if your account has never seen them.
Remove
Ends that one sequence. They stay on the lead list, so a future import can re-enrol them.
Suppress
Blocks the address account-wide and pulls them out of every campaign still running. This is the one that sticks.

Someone already in another active campaign bound to a different lead list can't be added — the server blocks it, and the extension shows you why.
