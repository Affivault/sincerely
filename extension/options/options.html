Reference

Three actions that are easy to confuse.

Add

Puts someone on a lead list, creating the contact first if your account has never seen them. Campaigns draw from lists, so this is what gets them emailed.

Remove

Takes them off that one list. They stay in your contacts, and stay on any other list.

Suppress

Blocks the address account-wide and takes them off every list. This is the one that sticks.

Adding is idempotent — someone already on the list is left alone rather than duplicated, so re-running a scan is always safe.
